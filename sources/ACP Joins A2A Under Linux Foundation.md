# ACP Joins A2A Under Linux Foundation

## Summary

- August 29, 2025: IBM's Agent Communication Protocol (ACP, launched March 2025 to power BeeAI and donated to the Linux Foundation that same month) officially merged into A2A.
- The ACP team stated it "will be winding down active development" and contributing technology and expertise directly to A2A — an explicit protocol-consolidation event, not a coexistence arrangement.
- Kate Blair (IBM) joined the A2A Technical Steering Committee alongside representatives from Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cisco, Salesforce, ServiceNow, and SAP.
- BeeAI users were given A2AServer/A2AAgent adapters and an ACP-to-A2A migration guide rather than a compatibility layer in the spec.
- ACP's total lifespan as an independent protocol was roughly 5 months (March-August 2025) — a concrete datapoint that foundation hosting does not guarantee protocol survival.
