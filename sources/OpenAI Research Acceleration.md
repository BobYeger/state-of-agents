# OpenAI Research Acceleration

## Summary

- By mid-August, OpenAI reports 3.1 agent workdays of runtime per human workday in its research organization. Median researcher inference exceeded $600 daily at API prices; these are usage measures, not equivalent human output or actual internal bills.
- Experiments per active experimenter increased alongside agent adoption and available compute. Delegation expanded into troubleshooting and monitoring, while high-level planning remained a small share of agent output.
- Over half of successful tasks estimated at 4–8 human hours required at least one intervention. OpenAI's claimed research-intern milestone means executing defined tasks under human direction.

## Evidence Boundary

Internal observational data cannot isolate causal research acceleration. Agentic classifiers infer task difficulty and outcomes; uncertain outcomes are excluded. The researcher population includes infrastructure and other support roles, and tool coverage is incomplete. Runtime, tokens, code, and experiment counts have different relationships to useful research progress.

## Report Implication

Evaluate research loops using verified outcomes, human intervention, and bottlenecks across the full workflow. More experiments can leave idea selection, validation, integration, and compute as the limiting steps; delegated activity alone does not demonstrate autonomous recursive improvement.
