# Instinct Personal Assistant

## Summary

- Consumer personal assistant reached by text or voice, with access to connected applications and devices. The [official product page](https://instinct.com/) describes email, messaging, screen, audio and location context, phone/computer operation, proactive follow-up, and tasks such as arranging rides or booking home services.
- This supports classification as a [[concepts/persistent personal agents|persistent personal agent]] at the product level. The user supplies access and priorities; the assistant is presented as acting across everyday tasks over time. The page does not describe its memory storage or retrieval mechanism.

## Evidence and Limits

- The [privacy policy](https://instinct.com/privacy-policy), revised August 26, 2026, identifies the operator and describes permission-dependent access to connected context. It names Google Workspace integrations including Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Tasks; access can be revoked, with indexed-data deletion separately available. These disclosures establish integration scope, not measured reliability.
- The homepage says its core model is trained for personal tasks, but does not name a base model, harness, memory algorithm, scheduler, evaluation suite or research paper. Do not infer implementation from the founder's research history or similarly named research systems.
- Availability is incompletely verified: the public page offers “Text Instinct to get started”; the app exposed no readable onboarding state during this audit. The policy's August 26 revision is **not** evidence of a launch date. A founder announcement linked from a third-party mirror could not be verified at its primary X URL; neither unrestricted availability nor current invite-only status is established here.
- Product capability statements are vendor claims. No comparative success, intervention, cost or proactive-notification measurements were found in these primary pages.
