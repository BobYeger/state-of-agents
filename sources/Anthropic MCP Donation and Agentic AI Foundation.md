# Anthropic MCP Donation and Agentic AI Foundation

## Summary

- December 9, 2025: Anthropic donated MCP to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), a directed fund under the Linux Foundation co-founded by Anthropic, Block, and OpenAI, with support from Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, and Bloomberg.
- Adoption at donation time: 10,000+ active public MCP servers, 97M+ monthly SDK downloads (Python + TypeScript), 75+ connectors in Claude's directory.
- AAIF's other founding projects: goose (Block) and AGENTS.md (OpenAI).
- MCP's existing maintainer-led governance model remains unchanged; the AAIF board handles funding and membership while projects keep technical autonomy.
- Completes the 2025 donation trilogy: A2A to the Linux Foundation in June, IBM's ACP merged into A2A in August, MCP to AAIF in December.
