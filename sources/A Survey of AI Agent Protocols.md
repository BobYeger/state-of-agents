# A Survey of AI Agent Protocols

## Summary

- First comprehensive survey of agent protocols; proposes a two-dimensional taxonomy crossing context-oriented vs inter-agent protocols with general-purpose vs domain-specific protocols.
- Includes a comparative performance analysis of protocols across security, scalability, and latency dimensions.
- Identifies next-generation protocol requirements: adaptability, privacy preservation, group-based interaction, layered architectures, and collective-intelligence infrastructures.
- v1 2025-04-23; v3 (latest) 2025-06-21. Companion repository: zoe-yyx/Awesome-AIAgent-Protocol.
