# Record & Replay

## Summary

- Official Codex docs for demonstrating a recurring macOS workflow once and turning it into a reusable skill.
- Important because it converts demonstration into an inspectable and editable `SKILL.md`, moving tacit GUI/workflow knowledge into a reusable agent procedure.
- Fits the harness-engineering stack as procedural memory: repeated work becomes a maintained skill rather than a prompt repeated by a human.
- Distinct from workflow orchestration: Record & Replay captures a demonstrated procedure; workflows coordinate execution.
