# Constitutional Classifiers

## Summary

- Input/output classifiers trained on synthetic data generated by prompting LLMs with a natural-language constitution specifying permitted versus restricted content.
- Over 3,000 estimated hours of human red teaming found no universal jailbreak that extracted information at detail comparable to an unguarded model on most target queries.
- Deployment cost is quantified: an absolute 0.38% increase in production-traffic refusals and 23.7% inference overhead.
- Enhanced classifiers showed robust defense against held-out domain-specific jailbreaks in automated evaluations.
- Rare public accounting of what a production classifier layer costs and catches (red-team hours, refusal delta, inference overhead).
