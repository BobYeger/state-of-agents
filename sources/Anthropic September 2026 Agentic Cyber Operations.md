# Anthropic September 2026 Agentic Cyber Operations

## Coordination and Persistence

For GTG-10007, Anthropic describes a lead agent dividing intrusion work among parallel subagents. Persistent records retain targets, credentials, state, and instructions. Thirteen scheduled agents gather public-source material; these are not thirteen intrusion agents. Workstreams share tooling and infrastructure. [Primary cyber section](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026#cyber-operations-sep-26)

## Human Role and Evidence Limits

Humans develop workflows, select targets, review outputs, and participate in intrusions. GTG-20006 operators also refine automation skills. These are malicious-operator deployments, not spontaneous rogue-model incidents.

GTG-10007 targeted roughly fifty organizations, with data access separately described at education, retail, and government targets. Fifty is not a confirmed victim count. Chinese-speaking operators likely in Hunan is Anthropic's attribution assessment; state direction and independent victim confirmation remain unestablished here.

Published September 10, the report covers disruptions through August, not October events. Novelty-selected cases, provider visibility, incomplete public traces, and absent controlled baselines limit prevalence and uplift estimates.
