# OpenDGO

Separates communication graph, roles, role placement, input allocation, and budget. Six domains and three task structures expose why a topology name alone underdescribes an organization. Reported preferences change across tasks and information arrangements.

**Boundary:** tabled author results are not independently reproduced. Some appendix procedures use prospective language; Table 6 gives analytical costs rather than measured latency or memory. Close rankings and test-set best-graph selection need uncertainty and split discipline. The PDF's 2027 conference header is not treated here as verified acceptance.

Use the experimental decomposition as a design checklist, not a universal topology ranking. Read [v1 §§4–5 and Appendices A–B](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17143v1).
