# DORA State of AI-assisted Software Development 2025

## Summary

- Annual DORA report, renamed from "Accelerate State of DevOps" to "State of AI-assisted Software Development"; based on nearly 5,000 survey respondents plus 100+ hours of qualitative interviews, published 2025-09-24.
- 90% of respondents use AI at work (up 14 points year over year); over 80% believe AI increased their productivity, yet 30% report little or no trust in AI-generated code.
- First year in which AI adoption positively correlates with software delivery throughput — but it still correlates negatively with delivery stability (more change failures and rework) absent controls such as automated testing, version control hygiene, and fast feedback loops.
- Introduces the DORA AI Capabilities Model: seven organizational capabilities (clear AI stance, healthy data ecosystems, strong version control, working in small batches, user-centricity, quality internal platforms, and others) that amplify AI's positive effects.
- Defines seven team archetypes from "Foundational Challenges" (low performance, high instability and burnout) to "Harmonious High Achievers"; 90% of organizations have adopted at least one internal platform.
- Central thesis: "AI is an amplifier" — it magnifies existing organizational strengths and dysfunctions rather than fixing broken engineering systems.

## Claims

- [[claims/Claim - Runtime control and verification improve agent reliability]] — the throughput gain only holds without stability loss when verification controls (automated testing, fast feedback) are in place.
