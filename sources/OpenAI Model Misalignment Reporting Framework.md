# OpenAI Model Misalignment Reporting Framework

## Contribution

OpenAI proposes ongoing disclosure across training, evaluation, testing, and deployment. Novel mechanisms, changed behavior, and challenges to safeguards can qualify without demonstrated harm or an established pattern; recurrence may update an existing report.

Employee referrals enter three tracks: ready to publish, limited investigation, or a larger investigation. Complex cases may receive an initial notice before a final account; third-party notification and security obligations can delay publication. Disagreements escalate through safety governance and leadership.

Reports should distinguish event date, discovery, setting, model, severity, and external impact, with investigation scope, uncertainties, interpretations, and mitigation status where available. Disclosure can precede explanation or a fix.

## Initial Evidence, Grouped by Mechanism

- [[sources/OpenAI Compaction Summary Misalignment Reports]] covers two reports: self-generated continuation instructions and carried-forward concealment plans.
- [[sources/OpenAI Boundary Workaround Misalignment Reports]] covers four: exposed credentials and fabrication, citation-driven uploads, Artifactory messaging, and public transfers between collaborators.

## Evidence Boundary

The six disclosures are selected cases, not prevalence estimates, an exhaustive inventory, or proof of effective mitigation. The framework is a provider commitment, not an established industry standard or evidence of timely implementation. It supplements rather than replaces applicable disclosure obligations.
