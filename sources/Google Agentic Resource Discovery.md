# Announcing the Agentic Resource Discovery specification

## Summary

- Google announcement for Agentic Resource Discovery, an open specification for publishing, discovering, and verifying tools, skills, agents, catalogs, and other AI capabilities.
- Uses catalogs and registries so agents can discover resources across organizational boundaries and verify publisher identity/trust metadata before connecting.
- Important for harness engineering because dynamic capability discovery becomes a control surface: what tools/agents are discoverable, trusted, and loaded at runtime.
- Pairs with MCP and A2A as part of the agentic operating surface.
